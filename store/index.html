What are you saving for?

Every blaze you earn is worth 50 cents here. There are things: sports, skate, animals, style, gross science, games, tools, books, and more, all built to last. And there are experiences: lunch with a guide, hosting a party for your crew, a morning shadowing a pro, a night under the stars. Add anything to your list and watch the bars fill up.

Prices for things are typical US retail in November 2026, rounded to the nearest dollar; the real cost on redemption day may differ a little. Items marked with a warning need a guide: knives and the multi-tool require Wilderland's knife certification, the fire steel and model rockets are used only with a guide present, and the slackline and scooter follow the school's safety rules. Nothing here is a blind box, a surprise egg or a collectible pack: every item is exactly what it says.

How experiences are priced. Each one says what it takes. Its price is the cash it costs (food, supplies, tickets) at 2 blazes a dollar, plus 1 blaze for every minute of a guide's time beyond normal supervision, and half again for minutes that take a guide away from their crew or happen outside school hours. So a lunch with a guide is about $8 of pizza (16 blazes) plus 30 minutes of a guide who needs someone to cover for them (45 blazes). Guides decide when each experience can happen; the limit on each card says how often.

The grade buttons show what kids in each grade band tend to want most. Every student can save anything in the store.

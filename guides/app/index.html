Wilderland Guides
for iPhone and Android

Put Wilderland Guides on your phone

Add the Guides site to your Home Screen and it opens like an app: full screen, its own icon, one tap from your students, Life Skills, Behavior, Trail Partner and the handbook. Nothing to download from an app store, and it updates itself whenever the site does.

You're in the app. You're all set.

iPhone

  1. Open explore.wilderland.academy/guides in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow). On iOS 26, tap ••• next to the address bar first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, tap More.
  4. Leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  5. Open Guides from your Home Screen and sign in with your Wilderland Google account. You only do this once: the app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari.

Android

  1. Open explore.wilderland.academy/guides in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen (or Install app), then Install.
  3. Open Guides from your home screen or app list and sign in with your Wilderland Google account.

Good to know

  • Everything you can do on the site works in the app, with the same sign-in and the same data.
  • To remove it, press and hold the icon and choose Remove App (iPhone) or Uninstall (Android).
  • If signing in doesn't work in the app, use the site in Safari or Chrome for now and tell Alex.

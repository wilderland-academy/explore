Prices are typical US retail in November 2026, rounded to the nearest dollar; the real cost on redemption day may differ a little. Items marked with a warning need a guide: knives and the multi-tool require Wilderland's knife certification, the fire steel and model rockets are used only with a guide present, and the slackline and scooter follow the school's safety rules. Nothing here is a blind box, a surprise egg or a collectible pack: every item is exactly what it says.

Experiences are redeemed with a guide, who picks the day. Anything that involves a guide getting pied, taped, soaked or dressed up only happens with guides who signed up for it. Prices for experiences are set by how much kids want them and how rare they are, and never below what they cost the school.

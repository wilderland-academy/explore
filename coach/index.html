What about academics?

Mr. Fox doesn't help with academic work. AI that hands out answers makes it easy to skip the thinking, and the thinking is the point. We're trying an academics coach with a few students first: it only gives hints and questions, never answers, and guides read every chat.

Why ask Mr. Fox (an AI coach), not just a guide?

  • It's always there.Your guides help a whole crew at once. The coach can help you right now: when every guide is busy, before school, or at home.
  • It knows something about almost everything.Knots, budgets, birds, speeches, fractions. Whatever your challenge, Lab or project is about, you can ask.
  • Your guide still matters most.Only a guide (or a parent, for some challenges) can pass you. Take anything big, risky or personal to a real adult.

Four things to remember

  • It's a computer program.Not a person or a friend. It can be wrong, so check big things with your guide.
  • You do the thinking.It asks questions and gives hints. The work, and the pass, are yours.
  • Keep private things private.Never type your full name, address, phone, school or passwords.
  • Guides can read every chat.If something is wrong or you feel unsafe, tell a real adult. You can also use SafeUT or call or text 988.

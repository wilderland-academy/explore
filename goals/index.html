My goals

Pick where you want to be this week, this month and by the end of the school year. Small weekly goals add up to big year goals. Your guide looks at these with you at your Summit Session, and your parents can see them too.

Your goals are saved to your school account when you're signed in, so they follow you to any Chromebook. Your guides and your parents can see them; other students can't. Life Skills goals fill in by themselves when your guide records a pass. For app goals, type in how far you are, using the numbers in each app or on Basecamp.
